Shubham Khedaskar is a self-made game developer forged through years of independent grind, technical obsession, and a relentless drive to create experiences that feel alive. Not built by a studio — built by will.

With over 6 years deep in Unity development, Shubham architects complete games from the ground up — gameplay systems, open-world environments, character animations, UI flows, and mobile optimization — entirely solo.

His aesthetic: stylized, atmospheric worlds that blur the line between game and film. His method: build it until it feels like it was always meant to exist.

From viral Android releases on Google Play to atmospheric horror on Steam — each project is a chapter in a developer universe that grows with every release.
